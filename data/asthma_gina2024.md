# GINA 2024 — Adult Asthma Stepwise Management (Track 1, ICS-Formoterol)

## Assessment

Diagnosis requires a history of variable respiratory symptoms with variable
expiratory airflow limitation (spirometry with reversibility ≥ 12% and 200 mL,
peak-flow variability, or bronchial-challenge testing).

## Track 1 — preferred reliever: as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol

- Step 1 (symptoms < twice/month, no risk factors):
  as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol.
- Step 2 (symptoms ≥ twice/month): as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol.
- Step 3: low-dose maintenance ICS-formoterol + as-needed ICS-formoterol
  (MART regimen).
- Step 4: medium-dose maintenance ICS-formoterol + as-needed ICS-formoterol.
- Step 5: high-dose ICS-formoterol plus add-on therapy (LAMA, biologic,
  low-dose OCS as last resort). Refer for phenotypic assessment.

## Track 2 — alternative reliever (SABA)

Use SABA as reliever only if adherence to daily ICS is assured. Steps mirror
Track 1 with maintenance ICS + SABA reliever.

## Exacerbation management

- Mild-moderate at home: 4–10 puffs SABA every 20 min for up to 1 hour, or
  ICS-formoterol reliever up to max 72 mcg formoterol/day.
- ED / hospital: nebulised salbutamol + ipratropium, systemic corticosteroids
  (prednisolone 40–50 mg PO for 5–7 days), controlled O2 to SpO2 93–95%.
- Consider IV magnesium sulfate 2 g over 20 min in severe exacerbations not
  responding to initial therapy.

## Follow-up

Review response 1–2 weeks after any exacerbation; reassess inhaler technique,
adherence, environmental triggers, and comorbidities (allergic rhinitis, GERD,
obesity, OSA).
